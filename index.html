The free group F2 = ⟨a, b⟩

Every junction is a reduced word. A step moves the viewpoint to a neighbour: its branches grow to full size while the old centre shrinks away in the opposite direction. The tree looks the same from every junction, so each step is a symmetry of the whole graph.
